Safety boundary: legacy XLSX files are never imported directly. Uploaded workbooks are first analysed: every sheet is counted and profiled before anything is mapped. Transaction imports only run through the ERP's domain services; until an adapter is approved they stay locked (validated but not imported) rather than bypassing stock, ledger, or account logic.
Uploading a whole-database export (e.g. ALLEXPORT-*.xlsx with one sheet per table)? Upload it here with AUTO and the discovery layer will show every sheet, its real row count, the entity it looks like, and what can be adapted. To restore such a file table-for-table instead, use Import & Export → Full Raw Import.

Schema-to-template mapping matrix

Clients → Client (name, phone, address); Suppliers → Supplier; Materials → Material (category, unit, unit price); Accounts → Account (category, type, opening balance). GRN, bookings, sales, deliveries, payments, expenses and opening balances use the current domain vocabulary; they are validated and reconciled, and imported only through the application's own domain services. Legacy column names (Customer/Party, MOBILE NO, Qty/Bags, Rate/Price, Bill No/Invoice No, …) are normalised by the discovery layer — the mapping configuration in app/services/migration_discovery.py is extensible for future legacy files.
